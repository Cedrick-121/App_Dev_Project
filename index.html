Have questions?

Who can use CampusFlow?

All currently enrolled students at STI College Global City have access to the platform.

Can I book an appointment on the weekend?

No, the system strictly follows campus operating hours (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM).

What happens if I miss my schedule?

You will need to book a new appointment. Missed appointments are automatically voided at the end of the day.
